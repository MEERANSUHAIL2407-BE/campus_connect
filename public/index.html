About Campus Connect

Modernizing academic collaboration and college communication.

Campus Connect is designed to solve campus fragmentation. Instead of juggling multiple notice boards, WhatsApp groups, and dispersed emails, every member of our college community has a dedicated role-based space to interact, stay updated, and participate in academic and extracurricular life.

Core Platform Features

Everything you need for an engaged and organized campus experience.

📢

Instant Announcements

Stay informed with urgent academic, examination, placement, and emergency broadcasts posted directly by verified faculty and administration.

📅

Event Management

Explore technical hackathons, cultural fests, seminars, and workshops. Register instantly with single-click seat booking and deadline tracking.

🤝

College Clubs & Societies

Discover student-led clubs ranging from coding to robotics and arts. Join communities, connect with coordinators, and attend weekly meetups.

📚

Academic Resource Hub

Access notes, previous year question papers, lab manuals, and PDFs filtered conveniently by department, year, and subject.

🔍

Lost & Found Portal

Report misplaced or found campus items with descriptions, locations, and direct contact details to help campus members recover belongings.

💬

Direct Feedback & Reviews

Submit suggestions or grievances regarding infrastructure, canteen, or academics with transparent admin status updates and anonymous options.

How Campus Connect Works

Three streamlined roles working in unison.

1. Students

Register with your student ID, browse campus news, sign up for exciting events, join clubs, download study notes, and engage with campus life.

2. Faculty

Publish departmental notices, host and monitor student event registrations, manage academic club activities, and upload course study materials.

3. Administrators

Oversee overall campus analytics, manage user roles, publish campus-wide alert notifications, and resolve student feedback transparently.
